Sales Report – Delivery Date (Commitment Date)

Add Commitment Date to Sales Pivot Report for better delivery tracking

Overview

This module enhances the Sales Analysis report in Odoo by adding the Commitment Date (Delivery Date) field to the Sales Pivot Report. With this addition, you can analyze and group your sales data based on delivery dates, improving delivery performance tracking and planning.

Key Features

  • Adds Commitment Date (Delivery Date) to the Sales Analysis Pivot Report.
  • Allows grouping and filtering sales by delivery date.
  • Helps track and plan delivery performance more effectively.
  • Easy installation and minimal configuration required.

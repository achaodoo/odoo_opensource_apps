Statement of Account (SOA) Report

Generate customer Statements of Account in PDF and Excel with ease.

SOA Report helps you generate a clear Statement of Account for a selected customer as of a specific date. It shows outstanding transactions, balances, credit days, and ageing details in an easy-to-read format.

Key Features

👤 Customer Selection

Select the required customer and generate the Statement of Account.

📅 As-On Date

Generate the report based on a specific As On Date.

📄 PDF Report

Generate a professionally formatted PDF Statement of Account.

📊 Excel Report

Export the Statement of Account in XLSX format for further analysis.

✉️ Send by Email

Send the Statement of Account directly to the customer with the PDF attached.

📈 Ageing Details

View balances across 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 91-180, 181-360, and above 360 days.

How It Works

  1. Open Invoicing → Reporting → SOA Report.
  2. Select the required Customer.
  3. Select the required As On Date.
  4. Choose Generate PDF Report, Generate Excel Report, or Send Email.
